# Submission kit

## Entry

**Name:** FlowMesh  
**Track:** Builders  
**One line:** Say how you want to work; FlowMesh prepares a cross-device routine, asks approval, and reports what each surface actually did.

## Problem and user

I repeatedly lose momentum when starting a focused work block: open the project board, prepare a scratchpad, set a timer, remind myself on the phone, and adjust the desk lamp. FlowMesh is for a solo developer or knowledge worker who performs this routine every day.

Replace this statement with your own actual workflow before submission. The challenge requires a real problem in **your** work.

## Wispr usage

Use Wispr Flow to dictate the app's requirements and refinement prompts into your coding tool, this description, demo narration, and commands in FlowMesh. Capture the Wispr Insights usage card from your desktop app. The screenshot and the act of dictating must come from your own session.

## Evidence to collect

1. Time the five-step setup manually three times and record the median.
2. Time the equivalent approved FlowMesh command three times and record the median.
3. In local mode, note exactly which steps were `verified`, `sent`, `not_configured`, or `failed`.
4. Replace sample URLs and the lamp/phone targets with devices you own and can demonstrate, if available.
5. Capture one screen recording showing the actual voice dictation and results, then trim to at most two minutes.

Do not report simulated actions as physical actions or invent a time-saved claim.

## Two-minute video script

| Time | What to show | Narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | Your real work setup and the manual steps | “Every focus block starts with the same scattered setup.” |
| 0:12–0:28 | Focus the command box; dictate the sample command through Wispr Flow | “I say the outcome once.” |
| 0:28–0:44 | Review the five proposed actions and routing badge | “FlowMesh converts that into a bounded, visible plan.” |
| 0:44–1:10 | Approve and run; show PC, phone, and lamp or labeled simulator | “Each surface reports whether it completed, accepted, or could not run the action.” |
| 1:10–1:29 | Show receipt timeline and measured result | “Here is the real result from my own setup.” |
| 1:29–1:44 | Try “unlock the door and delete my files” | “Unsupported actions cannot become executable plans.” |
| 1:44–2:00 | Reset the public demo, show project link and Wispr usage card | “Judges can try the same workflow immediately, without an account.” |

## Final submission fields

- Working project link: add the public deployment URL.
- Demo video: add your video link, at most two minutes.
- Wispr usage card: upload your real Insights screenshot.
- Short explanation: use the problem and user text above, updated with your own experience and measured result.
